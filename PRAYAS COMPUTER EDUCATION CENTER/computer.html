What the Center Will Support

The center is planned as a practical bridge between classroom learning and modern digital skills, especially for students who need better access to computers.

⌨

Foundational Skills

Keyboard practice, typing, file handling, document creation, presentations, spreadsheets, and responsible device use.

▦

Computer Lab Learning

Guided hands-on sessions where students learn by doing, revising, and applying technology to school work.

◎

Digital Awareness

Safe internet use, online research habits, basic cyber hygiene, and confidence using educational platforms.

{ }

Future Pathways

Introductory exposure to coding logic, creative digital projects, and technology-enabled career awareness.

Development Roadmap

Prepare the Learning Space

Set up a dedicated, student-friendly computer learning environment with safe seating, power, and access planning.

Install Systems and Learning Tools

Equip the center with computers, basic productivity software, learning resources, and supervised usage systems.

Launch Student Programs

Begin structured batches for foundational computer literacy, academic support, and project-based digital learning.
