Royalty Resolution Regarding Mr. Soneswar Narah

As per the agreement that came into effect in 2019, Prayas Adhyayan Chakra (PAC) is liable to pay a royalty of 10% to Mr. Soneswar Narah in recognition of his donation of land assets and his lifelong contribution to the organisation. However, this provision was not implemented during the period from 2019 to 2025.

Beginning with the academic year 2026-27, the aforementioned royalty shall be collected from the annual fees generated by the schools operated by PAC.

In keeping with the grants, investments, and personal contributions made by Mr. Narah since the inception of PAC in 2004, the royalty amount that would have accrued between 2019 and 2025 has been formally re-dedicated to the institution itself. Nevertheless, the resolution to formally allocate the agreed royalty shall take effect prospectively, starting from the budget of the academic year 2026-27 onwards.

This royalty entitlement shall continue to be payable to the nominated family member of Mr. Narah even after his demise.
